# CLI command tree, 11 Oct 2026

The CLI had 35 top-level commands in three grammars: verb first (`add`, `list`), noun first (`po create`), and flag combinations (`add -v X -p Y -q 9`). It now has 18 nouns, each with its own verbs: `buylog <noun> <verb>`.

## Decisions

- **No aliases for the old names.** buylog is pre-1.0 and has one known user. Aliases would keep both trees in `--help` and in the tests. The table below is the upgrade path.
- **Fixed verbs:** `add`, `list`, `show`, `edit`, `rm`. A noun has only the verbs it supports. Extra verbs are actions on that noun: `vendor open`, `quote status`, `quote refresh`.
- **`report` stays a noun.** The plan was a `--html` flag on `compare` and `spend`. `report purchases` and `report vendors` have no text equivalent, so the flag would have covered 2 of 4 reports.
- **`alert` and `watchlist` became `watch`.** They were two tables for one threshold. See `261011-model.md`, section 2.
- **Targets stay as they were.** Brands, products, vendors, specs and lists are named. Quotes, notes and price targets use IDs. POs use their number. A resolver that accepts either was not needed for this change.
- **Errors.** Handlers let `ServiceError` reach `main`, which prints `Error: ...` and returns 1. This removed 54 of the 64 identical `print(f"Error: {e}")` blocks.

## TUI

- `check_action` disables keys that do nothing on the active tab, and the footer hides them. Example: `t` (status) and `f` (filter) work only on Quotes, `o` only on Vendors.
- `h`/`l` now reach Specs and POs. `Ctrl+1` to `Ctrl+9` switch to each of the 9 tabs.
- Alerts and Watchlist are one Targets tab. `w` on Products opens the target form with the selected product filled in.
- `s` is removed. `/` focuses search, and Enter runs it.
